## I.1 What this edition asserts, and on what

7. **Code, sensing, \(\Pi\), and the twenty-one charged accounts.** A stabilizer code of distance \(d\) covering the physical error channels; QND syndrome sampling with the declared latency; per-site sensor power threshold \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{sens}}\) and the quantum-speed-limit rate bound; the embedding \(\Pi\) of the logical boundary fragments into projection operators; and the twenty-one charged fault accounts with genuine conditional kernels, whose sum is bounded by \(\sum\delta\le10^{-6}\) per declared horizon.
