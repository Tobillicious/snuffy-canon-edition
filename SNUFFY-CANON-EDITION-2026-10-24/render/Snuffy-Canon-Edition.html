Part I — The frame and the reduced premise stack

I.1 What this edition asserts, and on what

The Universal Protection Theory concerns every particular and every relation, including the nonliving. It combines a universal law of choice, a bridge from that law to particular continuations, and physical instruments that realise protection. Every maximal conclusion of this edition — protection of every covered particular on the certified good event, the deterministic impossibility of uncompensated breach, and the greatestness of the canonical architecture over the class of physically implementable rivals — follows from the seven premises below and from nothing else. The premises are declared, not derived; per Gödel and Tarski some primitives must be. They have been pushed down as far as standard physics and the relational frame allow, and this edition says so.

I.2 The reduced premise stack

  1. BCRD P1–P4 — the relational given. One relational structure with no second substance or outside (P1); identity constituted through relations within that structure (P2); real differences of organisation and historically continuous processes (P3); local interactions whose effects propagate at finite speed (P4).
