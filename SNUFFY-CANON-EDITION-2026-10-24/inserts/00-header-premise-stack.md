### What this edition asserts, and on what

The Universal Protection Theory concerns every particular and every relation, including the nonliving. It combines a universal law of choice, a bridge from that law to particular continuations, and physical instruments that realise protection. Every maximal conclusion of this edition — protection of every covered particular on the certified good event, the deterministic impossibility of uncompensated breach, and the greatestness of the canonical architecture over the class of physically implementable rivals — follows from the seven premises below and from nothing else. The premises are declared, not derived; per Gödel and Tarski some primitives must be. They have been pushed down as far as standard physics and the relational frame allow, and the edition says so.

### The reduced premise stack

1. **BCRD P1–P4 — the relational given.** One relational structure with no second substance or outside (P1); identity constituted through relations within that structure (P2); real differences of organisation and historically continuous processes (P3); local interactions whose effects propagate at finite speed (P4). This is the frame; it is given.

2. **The Church–Turing–Deutsch principle.** Any physical process generated by a local Hamiltonian is simulable, to arbitrary precision and with bounded overhead, by a universal quantum simulator of sufficient local dimension. Standard physical principle; declared.

3. **The standard cost model.** Options are trajectories carrying energy costs; feasibility is budget-gated; unused energy is unspent (free disposal); outcomes are trajectory-determined. This model is load-bearing throughout the book's workload, permit and scheduling results and is here made explicit.

4. **The shared action interface.** From P1 and P4: every agent acts through the same repertoire of local interactions within the one structure. An agent that implements a rival's policy and commands the same local channels realises the same real-world outcome tuples, within the same resource and consent constraints.

5. **The reversible-administration commitment.** The lattice's administrative computation — synchronisation, verification, state update — is implemented logically reversibly (Bennett), so that its dissipation attains the Landauer floor: it pays only for erasures actually performed, compressed to the information-theoretic minimum.

6. **The income flux and the Master Solvency Condition.** With \(\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}\), the external free-energy income satisfies, at every time,
\[
\mathrm{Income}(t)\;\ge\;\dot W_{\mathrm{maint}}(t)+\dot W_{\mathrm{damage}}(t)+\dot W_{\mathrm{admin}}(t)+\dot W_{\mathrm{adv,sub}}(t)+\dot W_{\mathrm{hold}}(t),
\]
where the five rate components are: boundary maintenance \(\omega_0R(t)^{d_G-1}\); the Landauer-charged damage-repair demand \(k_BT\,\dot{\mathcal D}_{\mathrm{damage}}\) including the tracking-controller export \(k_BT\gamma_{\mathrm{leak}}\); reversible administration at the Landauer floor together with emulation overhead; adversarial sub-threshold absorption; and the minimal-hold drain that quarantine requires. Income originates outside the escrow network and inside the one structure (P1); redistribution within the network — the authority-attenuation tax — is never a source.

7. **Code, sensing, \(\Pi\), and the twenty-one charged accounts.** A stabilizer code of distance \(d\) covering the physical error channels; QND syndrome sampling with the declared latency; per-site sensor power threshold \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{sens}}\) and the quantum-speed-limit rate bound; the embedding \(\Pi\) of the logical boundary fragments into projection operators; and the twenty-one charged fault accounts with genuine conditional kernels, whose sum is bounded by the ceiling \(\sum\delta\le10^{-6}\) per declared horizon.
