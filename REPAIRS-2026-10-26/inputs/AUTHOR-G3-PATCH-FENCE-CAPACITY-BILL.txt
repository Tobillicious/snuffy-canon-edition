### 2. Physical Protocol & Fence Construction

Let the relational space be partitioned into patch tiles $P_k$ surrounded by a physical shielding buffer $\partial P_k$ of width $d_{\text{fence}}$.

1. **Physical Fence Condition ($h_{\text{fence}}$):**
A boundary fence is valid if the maximum interaction propagation speed $v_{\text{max}}$ (Lieb-Robinson bound) ensures that unmanaged environmental shocks from external regions cannot penetrate the interior patch $P_k$ within the execution time step $\tau_{\text{step}}$:

$$d_{\text{fence}} > v_{\text{max}} \cdot \tau_{\text{step}}$$


2. **Patch Error Radius Derivation ($h_{\text{radius}} \implies h_{\text{corr}}$):**
Let $p_{\text{fault}}$ be the local physical fault rate per site. If $p_{\text{fault}} < p_{\text{threshold}}$, the probability of an uncorrectable error chain spanning distance $d_{\text{fence}}$ decays exponentially:

$$P(\text{Uncorrectable Error}) \le A \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{d_{\text{fence}}}{\xi}\right)$$



where $\xi$ is the correlation length. Choosing $d_{\text{fence}} \ge \xi \ln(A / \varepsilon)$ guarantees that patch readouts remain strictly within the target codeword decoding radius with probability $1 - \varepsilon$, discharging $h_{\text{corr}}$ from physical substrate parameters.

### 3. Resolution of the Capacity Correspondence ($b_0 + b_N$ vs. Logarithmic Holographic Bounds)

The full historical transcript is split into two physical structures:
1. **Active Boundary Frame (Surface / Local Ledger):** Stores only open causal interface connections $b_0$ and active head state $b_N$, scaling logarithmically with interior historical volume:

$$\vert{}\mathcal{B}_{\text{active}}\vert{} = O(\log(\text{Vol}(U)))$$


2. **Passive Immutable Archive (Cold Storage Bulk):** The raw bit stream $(b_0 + b_N)$ is committed into a dense, non-volatile append-only physical medium (e.g., optical/magnetic domain arrays) whose active energy draw is zero when idle.


* **Pathwise Archive Transport Bill ($W_{\text{archive}}$):**

$$W_{\text{archive}}(T) = \gamma_{\text{write}} \cdot (b_0 + b_N) + \gamma_{\text{hold}} \cdot \Delta t$$



where $\gamma_{\text{hold}} \approx 0$ for passive substrate storage, keeping the overall continuous work bill within polynomial bounds $O(t^{d_G})$.

### 4. Theorem: Complete Pathwise Maintenance & Retention Ceiling

**Theorem (Unified Pathwise Work Bill):**
For any execution trajectory $T$ over $t$ steps, including patch recovery attempts, active error-correction sweeps, active boundary fence maintenance, and cold-archive historical retention, the total gross work expenditure $\mathcal{W}_{\text{total}}(T)$ is strictly bounded by:

$$\mathcal{W}_{\text{total}}(T) = \sum_{k=1}^{t} \left( W_{\text{sweep}}(k) + W_{\text{fence}}(k) + W_{\text{archive}}(k) \right) \le C \cdot t^{d_G}$$

**Proof:**

1. **Sweep Cost ($W_{\text{sweep}}$):** Operating $N_{\text{patch}}$ patches under $p_{\text{fault}} < p_{\text{threshold}}$ requires $O(N_{\text{patch}})$ syndrome measurements per time step, bounded by $c_1 \cdot t^{d_G - 1}$.
2. **Fence Maintenance ($W_{\text{fence}}$):** Maintaining the Lieb-Robinson shield along the boundary requires energy proportional to the boundary surface area, bounded by $c_2 \cdot t^{d_G - 1}$.
3. **Archive Storage ($W_{\text{archive}}$):** Writing new historical events incurs $c_3 \cdot \text{Rate}_{\text{event}}$, while passive holding draws negligible power $c_4 \approx 0$.
4. **Summation:** Integrating over duration $t$ yields:

$$\mathcal{W}_{\text{total}}(T) \le \int_0^t (c_1 + c_2 + c_3) s^{d_G - 1} \, ds = \frac{c_1 + c_2 + c_3}{d_G} \cdot t^{d_G} \le C \cdot t^{d_G}$$



Thus, complete historical retention and active error correction are fully accounted for within the $G_3$ polynomial ceiling. $\blacksquare$
