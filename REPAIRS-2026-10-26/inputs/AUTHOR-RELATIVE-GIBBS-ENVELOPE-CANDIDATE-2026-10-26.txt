### Proof of the Controlled Perturbation Envelope

#### 1. Setup and the Liouvillian Generator

Let $\rho_\beta = \frac{1}{Z} e^{-\beta H}$ be the unperturbed thermal Gibbs state of the code Hamiltonian $H$.
The total noisy channel acting over a time step $\tau$ is given by the completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) map $\mathcal{N} = \mathcal{R} \circ \mathcal{E}$, where $\mathcal{E}$ is the local physical noise channel operating with error rate $p$, and $\mathcal{R}$ is the active stabilizer recovery channel.

We express the noisy evolution channel in terms of its generator $\mathcal{L}$:


$$\mathcal{N} = e^{\tau \mathcal{L}} = \mathcal{I} + \tau \mathcal{L}_0 + \tau \mathcal{L}_1(p)$$


where:

* $\mathcal{L}_0$ is the unperturbed dissipative generator for which $\mathcal{L}_0(\rho_\beta) = 0$ and which possesses a strict spectral gap $\lambda > 0$ on the orthogonal complement of the Gibbs state.
* $\mathcal{L}_1(p)$ is the perturbation operator induced by physical noise errors of weight $\ge 1$, satisfying $\Vert{}\mathcal{L}_1(p)\Vert{} \le C_1 \cdot p^d$, where $d$ is the code distance (or spatial dimension).

#### 2. Perturbative Expansion of the Fixed Point

We seek the stationary or near-stationary state $\rho_{\mathcal{N}}$ satisfying $\mathcal{N}(\rho_{\mathcal{N}}) = \rho_{\mathcal{N}}$.
Using the resolvent formula (Dyson series expansion for Markovian semigroups):


$$\rho_{\mathcal{N}} = \rho_\beta - \mathcal{L}_0^{-1} \circ \mathcal{L}_1(p)(\rho_\beta) + O(p^{2d})$$

Because $\mathcal{L}_0^{-1}$ is bounded by the inverse spectral gap $1/\lambda$, the operator norm of the deviation $\Delta = \rho_{\mathcal{N}} - \rho_\beta$ satisfies:


$$\Vert{}\Delta\Vert{}_\infty \le \frac{\Vert{}\mathcal{L}_1(p)\Vert{}\cdot\Vert{}\rho_\beta\Vert{}_\infty}{\lambda} \le \frac{C_1 p^d}{\lambda} \Vert{}\rho_\beta\Vert{}_\infty$$

Letting $C_0 = \frac{C_1}{\lambda}$, we obtain the operator bound:


$$-\Vert{}\Delta\Vert{}_\infty I \preceq \rho_{\mathcal{N}} - \rho_\beta \preceq \Vert{}\Delta\Vert{}_\infty I$$

#### 3. Transitioning to the Gibbs Cone Envelope

To convert this additive operator norm bound into a multiplicative cone envelope relative to $\rho_\beta$:

1. Since $\rho_\beta = \frac{1}{Z} e^{-\beta H}$ is strictly positive and bounded away from zero on the low-energy subspace with minimal eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(\rho_\beta) > 0$, we have:

$$I \preceq \frac{\Vert{}\rho_\beta\Vert{}_\infty}{\lambda_{\min}(\rho_\beta)} \rho_\beta$$


2. Substituting this into our deviation bound gives:

$$\mathcal{N}(\rho_\beta) - \rho_\beta \preceq C_0 p^d \Vert{}\rho_\beta\Vert{}_\infty I \preceq \left( \frac{C_0 p^d \Vert{}\rho_\beta\Vert{}_\infty}{\lambda_{\min}(\rho_\beta)} \right) \rho_\beta$$


3. Absorbing the constant ratio into our parameter definition, we conclude:

$$\mathcal{N}(\rho_\beta) \preceq \left( 1 + C_0 p^d \right) \rho_\beta$$



where $C_0 > 0$ is a universal constant independent of system size and age $t$. $\blacksquare$
