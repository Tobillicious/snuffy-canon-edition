### Detailed Breakdown: Stream Three: Reset Debt & Amortization

#### 1. The Localized Reset Penalty

When a sweep encounters an uncorrectable decoding failure on shell $k$, the system triggers a localized partition rollback.

* **Per-Shell Reset Cost:** The computational and thermodynamic work required to perform this rollback on shell $k$ is proportional to its boundary surface area:

$$w_{\text{reset}}(k) = R \cdot k^{d_G - 1}$$



where $R$ is the fixed unit reset cost per boundary site.

#### 2. The Cumulative Expected Reset Debt

Because resets only occur when a decoding failure happens, the total expected reset debt $\mathcal{Q}_{\text{reset}}(t)$ accumulated up to age $t$ is weighted by the probability of failure $P_{\text{err}}(k)$ derived in Stream Two:


$$\mathcal{Q}_{\text{reset}}(t) = \sum_{k=1}^{t} w_{\text{reset}}(k) \cdot P_{\text{err}}(k) = \sum_{k=1}^{t} R \cdot k^{d_G - 1} \cdot P_{\text{err}}(k)$$

#### 3. Substituting the Exponential Suppression Bound

Substituting our exponential error suppression bound from Stream Two ($P_{\text{err}}(k) \le A \cdot e^{-\gamma \gamma_0 k}$) into the reset sum:


$$\mathcal{Q}_{\text{reset}}(t) \le R \cdot A \sum_{k=1}^{t} k^{d_G - 1} \exp\left( -\gamma \gamma_0 k \right)$$

#### 4. Proof of Infinite Series Convergence (`G3CompleteBill` / `SummonsAudit`)

* **Asymptotic Behavior:** For any positive decay exponent ($\lambda = \gamma \gamma_0 > 0$) and any shell dimension $d_G \ge 1$, the polynomial growth term $k^{d_G - 1}$ is strictly outpaced by the exponential decay term $e^{-\lambda k}$.
* **Series Bound:** As age $t \to \infty$, the partial sums converge to a finite limit independent of $t$:

$$\lim_{t \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^{t} k^{d_G - 1} e^{-\lambda k} = S_{\text{conv}} < \infty$$


* **Constant Absorption:** Consequently, the total expected reset debt is strictly bounded by a constant that does **not** grow with age:

$$\mathcal{Q}_{\text{reset}}(t) \le R \cdot A \cdot S_{\text{conv}} \equiv C_{\text{reset}} = O(1)$$
