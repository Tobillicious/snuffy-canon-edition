### Rigorous Proof of the Residual Bound ($\delta_{\text{joint}}$)

#### Part 1: Discrete Path Variation Bound (Total Variation Certificate)

Let $E_0$ and $E_{\text{act}}$ be two quantum instruments mapping an input space $\mathcal{L}$ to an output space $\mathcal{Q} \otimes \mathcal{H}$ (where $\mathcal{H}$ represents retained classical records and reference frames). Suppose both instruments share the exact same set of physical branch channels $\{V_\lambda\}_{\lambda}$ but differ in their joint probability distribution over measurement outcomes, control flags, and error paths, denoted $K_0(\lambda)$ and $K_{\text{act}}(\lambda)$.

1. **Instrument Decomposition:**
For any input state $\rho$, the nominal and actual instruments are expressed as:

$$\mathcal{E}_0(\rho) = \sum_\lambda K_0(\lambda) V_\lambda \rho V_\lambda^\dagger, \quad \mathcal{E}_{\text{act}}(\rho) = \sum_\lambda K_{\text{act}}(\lambda) V_\lambda \rho V_\lambda^\dagger$$


2. **Diamond Norm Contractivity:**
The diamond norm distance between the two instruments acting on any state coupled to an arbitrary reference system $R$ satisfies:

$$\Vert{}\mathcal{E}_{\text{act}} - \mathcal{E}_0\Vert{}_\diamond = \sup_{\Vert{}\rho_R\Vert{}_{1} = 1} \Vert{}(\mathcal{E}_{\text{act}} - \mathcal{E}_0) \otimes \text{id}_R (\rho_R)\Vert{}_1$$


3. **Triangle Inequality & Trace Norm Bound:**

$$\Vert{}(\mathcal{E}_{\text{act}} - \mathcal{E}_0)(\rho)\Vert{}_- = \left\Vert{} \sum_\lambda \left( K_{\text{act}}(\lambda) - K_0(\lambda) \right) V_\lambda \rho V_\lambda^\dagger \right\Vert{}_1 \le \sum_\lambda \vert{}K_{\text{act}}(\lambda) - K_0(\lambda)\vert{} \, \Vert{}V_\lambda \rho V_\lambda^\dagger\Vert{}_1$$



Since each branch channel is completely positive and trace-preserving (CPTP) or bounded such that $\Vert{}V_\lambda\Vert{}_\diamond \le 1$, and since $\text{Tr}(V_\lambda \rho V_\lambda^\dagger) \le 1$:

$$\Vert{}\mathcal{E}_{\text{act}} - \mathcal{E}_0\Vert{}_\diamond \le \sum_\lambda \vert{}K_{\text{act}}(\lambda) - K_0(\lambda)\vert{} = 2 \, \text{TV}(K_{\text{act}}, K_0)$$



**Q.E.D.** for the discrete path certificate.

#### Part 2: Continuous Generator Drift Bound (Duhamel Integral Certificate)

1. **Duhamel Expansion:**
The propagators $\mathcal{U}_0(t,0)$ and $\mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,0)$ satisfy the integral relation (Duhamel's formula):

$$\mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,0) - \mathcal{U}_0(t,0) = \int_{0}^{t} \mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,u) \, \mathcal{V}(u) \, \mathcal{U}_0(u,0) \, du$$


2. **Diamond Norm Submultiplicativity:**
Taking the diamond norm of both sides and using the property that CPTP propagators have unit diamond norm ($\Vert{}\mathcal{U}\Vert{}_\diamond = 1$):

$$\Vert{}\mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,0) - \mathcal{U}_0(t,0)\Vert{}_\diamond \le \int_{0}^{t} \Vert{}\mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,u)\Vert{}_\diamond \, \Vert{}\mathcal{V}(u)\Vert{}_\diamond \, \Vert{}\mathcal{U}_0(u,0)\Vert{}_\diamond \, du$$


$$\Vert{}\mathcal{U}_{\text{act}}(t,0) - \mathcal{U}_0(t,0)\Vert{}_\diamond \le \int_{0}^{t} \Vert{}\mathcal{V}(u)\Vert{}_\diamond \, du$$

$$\Vert{}\mathcal{V}(u)\Vert{}_\diamond \le \frac{2}{\hbar} \Vert{}H_{\text{cross}}(u)\Vert{} + 2 \gamma(u) \Vert{}L(u)\Vert{}^2 \le v_a$$

$$\delta_{\text{joint}} \le 2^a v_a$$
