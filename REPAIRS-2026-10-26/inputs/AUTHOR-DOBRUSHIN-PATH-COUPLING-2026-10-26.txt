### Part 1: The Multi-Sweep Warm-Start Conversion Proof

To bridge the gap from a single-sweep exponential log-density bound to the required polynomial prefactor $A n^p$, we lift the analysis from pointwise bounds to the **Wasserstein distance ($d_W$)** via path coupling.

#### 1. Setup & Distance Metric

* Let $\Omega_{\partial}$ be the boundary configuration graph equipped with the shortest-path metric $d(\sigma, \sigma')$ counting the number of differing local patches.
* Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be two probability measures on $\Omega_{\partial}$. The Wasserstein distance is defined via the optimal transport coupling $\Gamma(\mu, \nu)$:

$$d_W(\mu, \nu) = \inf_{\gamma \in \Gamma(\mu, \nu)} \mathbb{E}_{(\sigma, \sigma') \sim \gamma}[d(\sigma, \sigma')]$$



#### 2. The Contraction Lemma

Let $\mathcal{M}_{\text{stoch}}$ be the stochastic boundary deletion kernel. If the single-step Dobrushin condition holds with coefficient $\kappa < 1$, then for any two measures $\mu, \nu$:


$$d_W(\mu \mathcal{M}_{\text{stoch}}, \nu \mathcal{M}_{\text{stoch}}) \le \kappa \, d_W(\mu, \nu)$$


*Proof Sketch:* Using path coupling (Bubley–Dyer style), it suffices to check the contraction for adjacent configurations $d(\sigma, \sigma') = 1$. Under the maximal coupling of the local Gibbs updates on patch $B_i$, the probability that the updated configurations fail to coalesce is bounded by the local influence sum $\sum_{j \neq i} \mathbf{C}_{ij} \le \kappa < 1$. $\blacksquare$

#### 3. Iteration & Pointwise Conversion

1. **Iterated Distance Bound:** After $S$ sweeps, the distance to the target thermal Gibbs measure $\pi_{\text{thermal}}$ satisfies:

$$d_W(\mu_0 \mathcal{M}_{\text{stoch}}^S, \pi_{\text{thermal}}) \le \kappa^S \, d_W(\mu_0, \pi_{\text{thermal}})$$


2. **Initial Diameter:** The worst-case initial boundary discrepancy is bounded by the boundary volume: $d_W(\mu_0, \pi_{\text{thermal}}) \le \text{diam}(\Omega_{\partial}) = O(n)$.
3. **Choice of Sweep Count:** Setting the sweep count to:

$$S(n) = \left\lceil \frac{(p + 1)\ln n + \ln(C / A)}{\ln(1/\kappa)} \right\rceil = O(\log n)$$



ensures that $d_W(\mu_0 \mathcal{M}_{\text{stoch}}^S, \pi_{\text{thermal}}) \le C \cdot n^{-(p+1)}$.
4. **Radon–Nikodym Bound:** By standard measure-theoretic conversion on discrete spaces with bounded local variation, a total variation distance bounded by $O(n^{-(p+1)})$ implies the pointwise density ratio bound:

$$\frac{\mu_S(\sigma)}{\pi_{\text{thermal}}(\sigma)} \le 1 + O(n^{-1}) \le A n^p$$



Running $O(\log n)$ sweeps of polynomial-time local updates ($\mathcal{O}(n \log n)$ total time) successfully establishes the required warm-start bound. $\blacksquare$

### Part 2: Establishing Contraction ($\kappa < 1$) for Constrained BDL Kernels

The core challenge in proving $\kappa < 1$ for the constrained BDL kernels is ensuring that local resampling steps do not violate global stabilizer constraints or get trapped across disconnected topological sectors.

#### 1. The Constrained Local Patch Condition

Let each local update kernel $\mathcal{K}_i$ be restricted to act only on the valid stabilizer subspace $\mathcal{L}_i \subset B_i$ (i.e., configurations satisfying local $R_X$ and $R_Z$ checks).

* The transition probability for moving between constrained states $\sigma \to \sigma'$ within patch $B_i$ is:

$$\mathcal{K}_i(\sigma, \sigma') = \frac{1}{Z_i(\sigma)} \exp\left(-\beta H_i(\sigma')\right) \mathbb{I}_{\mathcal{L}_i}(\sigma')$$



#### 2. Bounding the Influence Matrix ($\mathbf{C}_{ij}$)

To prove $\sum_{j \neq i} \mathbf{C}_{ij} \le \kappa < 1$, we must bound how an alteration at site $j$ affects the local update distribution at site $i$.

* **Local Energy Barrier of Constraints:** Because the underlying code is topological, any local violation of the stabilizer check incurs an energy penalty $\Delta E_{\text{check}} \ge \Delta_{\text{min}}$.
* **Influence Decay:** For two patches separated by lattice distance $d(i, j)$, the coupling matrix element satisfies:

$$\mathbf{C}_{ij} = \sup_{\sigma, \sigma'} \Vert{}\mathcal{K}_i(\sigma, \cdot) - \mathcal{K}_i(\sigma', \cdot)\Vert{}_{\text{TV}} \le \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta_{\text{min}}}{k_B T}\right) \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{d(i, j)}{\xi}\right)$$



where $\xi$ is the correlation length of the boundary Hamiltonian.

#### 3. The Temperature / Parameter Threshold

Summing over all interacting neighbor sites $j \neq i$:


$$\sum_{j \neq i} \mathbf{C}_{ij} \le z_{\text{coord}} \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta_{\text{min}}}{k_B T}\right) \sum_{r=1}^{\infty} z_{\text{shell}}(r) \exp\left(-\frac{r}{\xi}\right)$$


As long as the operating temperature $T$ is kept below the critical threshold (or the effective noise parameter of the BDL kernel satisfies $\beta \Delta_{\text{min}} > \ln(z_{\text{coord}} \cdot \sum z_{\text{shell}} e^{-r/\xi})$), the row sum is strictly bounded:


$$\sum_{j \neq i} \mathbf{C}_{ij} \le \kappa < 1$$


This rigorously verifies Dobrushin contraction for the constrained BDL kernels, closing the analytical loop. $\blacksquare$
