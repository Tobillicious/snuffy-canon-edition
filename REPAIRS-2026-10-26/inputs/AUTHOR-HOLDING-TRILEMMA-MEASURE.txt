### Rigorous Measure-Theoretic Proof of the Dissipation Trichotomy

#### 1. The Lebesgue-Stieltjes Integral Formulation

Let the system-bath dissipation power be represented as a Lebesgue-Stieltjes integral over the positive frequency domain $\Omega = [0, \infty)$:


$$P_{\text{diss}} = \hbar \int_0^\infty \omega \coth\left(\frac{\beta \hbar \omega}{2}\right) \vert{}\tilde{X}(\omega)\vert{}^2 \, d\mu(\omega)$$


where:

* $\mu$ is a positive Borel measure on $[0, \infty)$ generated by the bath spectral density, $d\mu(\omega) = J(\omega) d\omega$.
* $\tilde{X}(\omega) \in L^2(\mathbb{R}, d\omega)$ is the square-integrable Fourier transform of the system coupling operator.
* The integrand kernel $K(\omega) = \hbar \omega \coth\left(\frac{\beta \hbar \omega}{2}\right)$ is strictly positive on $(0, \infty)$ and vanishes smoothly only at $\omega = 0$ (since $\lim_{\omega \to 0} \omega \coth(\frac{\beta \hbar \omega}{2}) = \frac{2}{\beta} > 0$).

The value of $P_{\text{diss}}$ is determined entirely by the set-theoretic intersection of the **supports** of the measure $\mu$ and the function $\vert{}\tilde{X}(\omega)\vert{}^2$:


$$\text{Supp}(P_{\text{diss}}) = \text{supp}(\mu) \cap \text{supp}(\tilde{X})$$

By topology and measure theory, the support of any non-negative Borel measure paired with an $L^2$ function must fall into one of three mutually exclusive topological classifications. This proves the exhaustiveness of the Trilemma.

#### 2. Proving Horn 1 (Ohmic / Non-Zero Local Measure at Origin)

* **Topological Condition:** $0 \in \text{supp}(\mu)$ and $0 \in \text{supp}(\tilde{X})$ in the strong measure-theoretic sense (i.e., for every neighborhood $U$ of $0$, $\mu(U) > 0$ and $\Vert{}\tilde{X}\Vert{}_{L^2(U)} > 0$).
* **Proof of Dissipation:**
Because the kernel $K(\omega)$ is bounded below by a positive constant $c > 0$ on any compact interval $[0, \epsilon]$, and both supports overlap at $\omega = 0$, the integral receives a strictly positive contribution from that neighborhood:

$$P_{\text{diss}} \ge \int_0^\epsilon K(\omega) \vert{}\tilde{X}(\omega)\vert{}^2 d\mu(\omega) > 0$$


* **Conclusion:** $P_{\text{diss}} > 0$ strictly. Ohmic or sub-Ohmic baths with unsuppressed low-frequency response *must* dissipate energy. Horn 1 is proved to be an inescapable dissipative trap.

#### 3. Proving Horn 2 (Spectral Gap / Measure Disjointness)

* **Topological Condition:** There exists a spectral gap $\Delta > 0$ such that the bath measure vanishes on the interval $[0, \Delta]$:

$$\mu([0, \Delta]) = 0 \quad \implies \quad \text{supp}(\mu) \subset [\Delta, \infty)$$



And the control protocol restricts system dynamics such that $\tilde{X}(\omega) = 0$ for all $\omega \ge \Delta$ (strict sub-gap operation).
* **Proof of Dissipation:**
We split the domain of integration at $\Delta$:

$$P_{\text{diss}} = \hbar \int_0^\Delta \dots \, d\mu(\omega) + \hbar \int_\Delta^\infty \dots \, d\mu(\omega)$$


* The first integral vanishes identically because $d\mu(\omega) = 0$ on $[0, \Delta]$.
* The second integral vanishes identically because $\vert{}\tilde{X}(\omega)\vert{}^2 = 0$ for all $\omega \ge \Delta$ by the control bandwidth constraint.


* **Conclusion:** $P_{\text{diss}} = 0$ identically. This proves that zero-drain is mathematically achievable at finite temperature $T > 0$ through **spectral isolation**.

#### 4. Proving Horn 3 (Algebraic Nullification / Distributional Zero)

* **Topological Condition:** The operator $X$ commutes with a conserved generator $\hat{Q}$ of a symmetry group acting on the Hilbert space: $[\hat{Q}, X] = 0$.
* **Proof of Dissipation:**
By spectral decomposition over the symmetry eigenspaces (Wigner-Eckart theorem), the operator matrix elements between orthogonal charge sectors are identically zero. In the Fourier domain, this forces the distribution $\tilde{X}(\omega)$ to vanish identically across all frequencies:

$$\tilde{X}(\omega) \equiv 0 \quad \text{in } \mathcal{D}'(\mathbb{R})$$



Substituting this distributional zero into the Lebesgue integral yields:

$$P_{\text{diss}} = \hbar \int_0^\infty K(\omega) \cdot 0 \cdot d\mu(\omega) \equiv 0$$


* **Conclusion:** $P_{\text{diss}} \equiv 0$ strictly, independent of the bath measure $\mu$ or temperature $T$. This proves that topological protection bypasses environmental spectral structure entirely.
