### Step 1: Topological Lower Bound on Creation Events ($k(\Gamma) \ge c \cdot d$)

#### 2. Topological Contour Decomposition

#### 3. Energy/Fault Accounting Across Boundaries

Across any inter-region boundary crossing $R_m \to R_{m'}$:

$$k(\Gamma) \ge \frac{1}{2} \cdot N_{\text{boundary}} \ge \frac{1}{2 \times 72} d = c \cdot d$$
