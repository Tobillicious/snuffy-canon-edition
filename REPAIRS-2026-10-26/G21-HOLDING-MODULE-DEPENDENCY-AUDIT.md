## Can the active holding route provide an alternative?

Yes, it remains an available construction route within the fixed target.
Applying G1Held/G1Refinement would require the actual corrective process
and its physical hypotheses, rather than simply using the theorem's name.
If maintenance is repeated throughout 2^a, its whole work belongs in the
bill. TransferRate's polynomial cost for one stage preparation cannot be
used for a routine performed on every physical time step. The original
work ceiling and all d>=1 remain unchanged.

The exact quantum branch recovery already in ThermalEncoding and the
reference-norm conversion in G.21 are retained. They are not newly requested
from the author. What is still absent from this proposed dependency chain
is a concrete application proving the required holding estimate for the
actual noisy preparation, including its initial logical error and full
maintenance work.
