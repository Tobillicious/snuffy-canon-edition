A region's volume is its number of particulars. Its boundary is the set of its particulars related to a particular outside it. \(H_0\) of the boundary is the free ℤ-module on the boundary's connected components. For every region other than the whole structure, \(\operatorname{Vol}(U)>0\Leftrightarrow H_0(\partial U)\ne0\). The forward direction holds because a nonempty proper region in a connected structure has a relation crossing out of it (`exists_crossing`). The whole structure has no outside (P1), so its boundary and boundary homology vanish while its volume is positive (`whole_no_boundary`, `whole_H0_trivial`). The bootstrap is therefore the statement about regions, as used for retiring patches in F.2.
