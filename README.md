## Evidence conventions

Three evidence kinds are distinguished and never conflated: **compiled** components (Lean receipts), **exact replay** receipts (the ledger's hash-chained traces and the banks' finite checks), and **prose derivations** at declared premises. A prose proof does not acquire a compiler certificate by association.
